### One important change I'd make

Don't tell Antigravity to treat the **ward GeoJSON as the actual latest ward configuration**. The India Geodata repository currently provides the Vijayawada boundary and ward files, but those are geographic datasets with their own source/version context. ([Yashveeeeeeer][2])

For the hackathon, I'd have the app label the geographic layer something like:

> **Vijayawada Administrative Wards — Demo Dataset**

rather than making a claim about the current official ward delimitation.

And for the **street map**, use an OSM extract rather than trying to derive roads from the ward/boundary GeoJSON. BBBike explicitly supports extracting OSM areas into formats such as GeoJSON, MBTiles and PMTiles. ([BBBike Extracts][1])

That separation will make your architecture much cleaner:

```text
Vijayawada Boundary
        │
        ├── Ward GeoJSON
        │
        └── OSM Road/Map Data
                  │
                  ▼
            Leaflet Map
                  │
        ┌─────────┴──────────┐
        ▼                    ▼
Static Geography       Live Demo State
                       ├── Emergencies
                       ├── Resources
                       ├── Shelters
                       ├── Hospitals
```
